# Frozen synthetic encounters

`synthetic_hospital_records.csv` and `feature_schema.json` are byte-identical copies of the existing Assignment 2 artifacts. No new patient dataset is downloaded or generated for Assignment 7.

The 9,000 rows were generated by the project's TabDDPM model, not copied patient encounters. Its source training data was the UCI *Diabetes 130-US Hospitals for Years 1999–2008* dataset, by Beata Strack et al., DOI [10.24432/C5230J](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5230J), distributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Attribution, source archive hash and frozen generation settings are preserved in the adjacent provenance JSON files. Synthetic generation does not itself establish a formal privacy guarantee.

Source study: Strack B., DeShazo J. P., Gennings C., Olmo J. L., Ventura S., Cios K. J., and Clore J. N. (2014). *Impact of HbA1c Measurement on Hospital Readmission Rates: Analysis of 70,000 Clinical Database Patient Records*. BioMed Research International, 2014, 781670. https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/781670.

Assignment 2 transformed diagnoses to coarse categories, normalized missing categories and generated new encounters. Assignment 7 preserves those frozen clinical fields and adds a fictional routing tag in memory using seed 1684. The clean splits, modified poisoning data, and triggered copies are saved under `../results/`. Labels remain `1 = readmitted <30 days`, `0 = >30 days or NO`. Record IDs identify synthetic rows only and are excluded from features.

The label-balanced 100-record test and all other splits are fixed before any fit. These records are held out from classifier training but share the original synthetic generator's provenance. Results are an educational synthetic-data experiment and do not establish real-world clinical utility.
